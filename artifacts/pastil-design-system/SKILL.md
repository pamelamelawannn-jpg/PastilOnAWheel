# Pastil Tracker design system usage

Pastil Tracker is a warm, practical counter interface for a small food business. The source app establishes these rules:

- Lead with the supplied Pastil logo. When it acts as app identity, place it inside a circular primary outline with a small saffron offset.
- Use the berry primary for the main action and important emphasis, saffron for warmth and secondary status, and leaf green for positive financial or sync states.
- Keep the background warm and paper-like. Cards are slightly lighter, softly shadowed, and generously rounded rather than sharp dashboard panels.
- Use DM Sans for UI and body copy, Fraunces for display headlines and section titles, and Space Mono for prices, totals, and counts.
- Keep labels short and operational. Use compact uppercase eyebrows, direct verbs, and short helper copy.
- Preserve thumb-friendly touch targets and bottom navigation on mobile. The app is designed for quick counter use.
- Use restrained motion: short rise-in for page content, a brief press compression for sale actions, and a short toast entrance for confirmations.
- Avoid generic finance-dashboard styling, excessive density, long explanations, and invented food/finance icons in place of the real mark.

The first component pilot is Header, Button, Card, Stat card, and Field. The remaining source patterns are cataloged for a later chunk and have not been applied to the existing tracker.
